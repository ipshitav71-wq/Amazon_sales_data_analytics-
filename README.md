# # 📊 Amazon Sales Data Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Amazon sales data across ship modes, countries, cities, product categories, and global markets.

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard provides a multi-dimensional view of Amazon's sales performance, helping stakeholders quickly identify:
- Which shipping modes drive the most sales
- Top-performing countries and cities
- Category-wise revenue contribution
- Market-wise sales distribution across regions

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Sales by Ship Mode
- **Standard Class** dominates with **7.6M** in sales, followed by Second Class (2.6M), First Class (1.8M), and Same Day (0.7M)
- Indicates most customers prioritize cost over delivery speed

### Sales by Country
- **United States** leads with **2.30M** in sales, contributing the largest share
- Followed by Australia (0.93M), France (0.86M), China (0.70M), and Germany (0.63M)

### Sales by City
- **New York City** is the top-performing city at **0.26M**
- Los Angeles (0.18M), Manila, Seattle, and San Francisco (0.12M–0.11M) round out the top cities

### Sales by Category
- **Furniture**: 4.11M (32.52%)
- **Technology**: 4.74M (37.53%)
- **Office Supplies**: 3.79M (29.96%)
- Technology is the highest-revenue category, closely followed by Furniture

### Sales by Market
- **APAC** leads all markets with **3.59M (28.36%)** in sales
- Followed by US (2.94M, 23.24%), EU (2.3M, 18.17%), LATAM (2.16M, 17.12%), EMEA (0.81M, 6.38%), and Africa (0.78M, 6.20%)

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** — Dashboard design, DAX measures, data visualization
- **DAX** — Sum aggregations across multiple dimensions
- Data visuals used: Area chart, Funnel chart, Bar chart, Pie chart, Donut chart
